# Fireflies.ai: From Meeting Notes to Closed Commitments

A product management case study exploring how Fireflies.ai could help teams maintain accurate meeting commitments and record honest outcomes without becoming another project-management tool.

## The problem

Fireflies can capture meeting action items, but work often continues in tools such as Jira, Teams, Slack, email and spreadsheets.

As ownership, deadlines and progress change, the original meeting record may no longer reflect the latest state. This can create manual follow-ups and make final outcomes difficult to verify.

## Research

The project used 34 research records:

- 23 survey responses
- 11 user interviews
- Product and competitor teardowns
- Secondary research from product documentation and public reviews

These are research records, not necessarily 34 unique participants. Participant identities and raw research materials are excluded from this public repository.

## Product decision

The proposed workflow focuses on:

- Host review of AI-extracted commitments
- Owner confirmation before accountability begins
- Quick updates when work remains open
- Explicit outcomes: Done, Reassigned or Deliberately Dropped
- Clear unresolved states without assuming completion

The solution improves commitment continuity without turning Fireflies into another project-management tool.

## Prototype scope

The interactive prototype demonstrates the proposed workflow using one sample commitment.

It does not include:

- Automatic Jira or external-tool synchronisation
- A production backend
- Live Fireflies data
- Formal usability-test results
- A complete project-management system
